# Fragrance Finder — project status

Last reviewed: 17 October 2026

## Purpose

Help users discover fragrances through a curated UK dataset and transparent similarity/recommendation logic, funded by clearly disclosed retailer affiliate commissions.

## Current state

A responsive static prototype exists with 100 launch fragrances and precomputed comparison relationships. Retailer-product imagery/links are handled through a separate temporary adapter.

The **affiliate-funded business model is confirmed** (17 October 2026). Product expansion and launch prioritisation remain undecided; no commercial affiliate partnership has been verified.

## What works

- 50 perfumes and 50 men's fragrances.
- Structured fragrance profiles.
- Five comparable recommendations per scent.
- 500 precomputed comparison relationships.
- Static responsive UI.
- Temporary retailer-product adapter.
- Vercel and GitHub Pages deployment paths.

## Live / deployment status

Deployment paths exist, but no active commercial/public launch is currently evidenced.

## Current milestone

**Product direction agreed; build priority still unassigned. Define and verify an affiliate partner/feed route before launch.**

## Key decisions

- Match scoring is transparent guidance, not scientific prediction.
- No dupe/performance guarantee.
- Core fragrance data stays separate from retailer-product feeds.
- Affiliate-funded website is the approved commercial direction. Keep discovery free and recommendation ranking independent of commission incentives.
- Disclose affiliate relationships and any sponsored placements; do not assume partner approval, valid commissions or verified prices without proof.

## Current blockers / dependencies

- Specific approved affiliate networks/retailers and referral arrangements.
- Sustainable concentration-matched product feeds, link reliability and imagery permissions.
- Disclosures, cookie/consent/tracking design and referral verification.
- Decision on whether the curated 100-fragrance launch dataset is enough for the intended product.

## Next actions if resumed

1. Shortlist affiliate programmes/retailers and check terms and commercial viability.
2. Verify permitted product feeds, images, concentration matching, prices and links.
3. Add clear affiliate disclosure and compliant attribution/consent where required.
4. Test retailer link behaviour and commission/referral tracking with approved partners.
5. Confirm catalogue breadth and decide when to prioritise a public commercial launch.

## Deferred / out of scope

Avoid expanding catalogues, accounts, social features or recommendation complexity before the affiliate partner/feed route and core conversion journey are validated.

## Handoff notes

The commercial model is **settled as affiliate-funded**, but the implementation is not yet a launched affiliate business. Keep unresolved partnerships and deployment evidence explicit.
